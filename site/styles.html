1 · Manualquiet, printed, serif headingsoption 1

utsugi-pmaports

postmarketOS for two Xiaomi phones, packaged so that a clean install becomes a working phone with one apk add.

Three levels

utsugi-surya-basekernel, audio, sensors, GPS, cameras, power
utsugi-surya-fullbase, plus the six applications
utsugi-surya-extrasbrowser, mail, maps, messaging
